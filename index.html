FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the project — a one-page site is quicker than a multi-page site or an online shop. You'll get a clear timeline together with your quote.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, any text and photos you already have, and a few examples of websites you like. I'll guide you through everything else.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. If you need to change content regularly, I'll set the site up so you can edit it easily and show you how.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Care & support covers updates, improvements and fixes so your website keeps performing.

How do we get started?

Send me an email with a short description of your business and what you need. I'll reply within 24 hours to arrange a call.
