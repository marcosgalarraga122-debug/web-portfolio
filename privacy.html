Marcos's AI Assistant (the chat)

The chat button opens an AI assistant: replies are written by an AI model, not by Marcos, and can contain mistakes. It only uses information about Marcos's services that he has provided.

  • What is stored: the messages you send and the replies you receive, their time, the language, the section of the page where you opened the chat, and whether you used a contact option offered in the chat. If you have accepted analytics (below), the conversation is also linked to your analytics ID.
  • What is not stored: your IP address or browser details. Please don't share sensitive information in the chat. If you choose to type your name or contact details, they are stored as part of the conversation.
  • Why: to answer your questions, and so Marcos can read the conversations to follow up on enquiries and improve the assistant. The basis is Marcos's legitimate interest in answering questions about his services. You can object at any time by email.
  • How long: conversations are deleted automatically 90 days after the last message, or earlier on request.
  • In your browser: while the tab is open, the conversation is kept in your browser's session storage so it isn't lost when you close the chat. This is needed for the chat to work and is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Who processes it: the chat runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers, D1 and Workers AI), which acts as Marcos's service provider. Conversations and analytics are stored in Cloudflare's EU data jurisdiction. To write a reply, the AI model may run in a Cloudflare data centre outside the EU; Cloudflare states that it does not keep the messages after answering and does not use them to train AI models.

Visit analytics (only if you accept)

If you click Accept in the cookie banner, the site records page views, opening the assistant, sending it a question and clicks on contact options, together with the page, the website you came from (its domain only), your device type (desktop, tablet or mobile), the site language and a random ID stored in your browser (local storage) to count unique visitors. Your IP address, browser fingerprint and location are not collected.

If you click Reject, nothing is recorded and the random ID is removed from your browser. The banner asks again on each visit, so you can change your choice at any time; you can also clear this site's data in your browser. Analytics records are deleted automatically after 12 months. They are processed on Cloudflare, as above.

Contact by email

The contact form and email links open your own email app; nothing is sent until you press send there. Marcos uses your email only to reply to you and keeps it as long as needed for that conversation or any resulting project.

Hosting and other services

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and some scripts from the public code libraries cdnjs (Cloudflare) and unpkg, which receive your IP address to deliver the files.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it, by emailing Marcos.galarraga122@outlook.com. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD.
